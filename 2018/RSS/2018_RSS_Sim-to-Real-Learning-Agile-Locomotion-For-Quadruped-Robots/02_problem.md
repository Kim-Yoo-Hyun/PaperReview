- I NTRODUCTION Designing agile locomotion for quadruped robots is a longstanding research problem .
- These algorithms can solve locomotion problems from scratch without much human intervention.
- This is because it is difficult to control an under-actuated robot performing highly dynamic motion that involve intricate balance.

## 해결하려는 문제
