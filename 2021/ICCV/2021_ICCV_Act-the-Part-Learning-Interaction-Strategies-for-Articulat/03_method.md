## Brief Method
- Conclusion and Future Work We present Act the Part (AtP) to take visual observations of articulated objects, interact strategically, and output part segmentation masks.
- To this end, we introduce Act the Part (AtP) to learn how to interact with articulated objects to discover and segment their pieces.
- Our method learns an efficient policy that chooses optimal actions (i.e., actions that discover new parts) more frequently than other approaches. (e.g., push new parts to discover them).
