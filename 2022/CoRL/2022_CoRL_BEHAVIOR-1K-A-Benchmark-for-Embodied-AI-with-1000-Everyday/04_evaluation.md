## Evaluation Protocol and Results
- Our experiments indicate that the activities in BEHAVIOR-1K are long-horizon and dependent on complex manipulation skills, both of which remain a challenge for even state-of-the-art robot learning solutions.
- BEHAVIOR-1K includes two components, guided and motivated by the results of an extensive survey on ‘what do you want robots to do for you?’.
