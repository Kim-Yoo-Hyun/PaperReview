## 왜 문제인가
- —This paper introduces DextAIRity, an approach to manipulate deformable objects using active airflow.
- In contrast to conventional contact-based quasi-static manipulations, DextAIRity allows the system to apply dense forces on out-ofcontact surfaces, expands the system’s reach range, and provides safe high-speed interactions.
- These properties are particularly advantageous when manipulating under-actuated deformable objects with large surface areas or volumes.
