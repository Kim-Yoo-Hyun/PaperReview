# Robotics-first tier reclassification — 2026-09-02

## Scope

This update reclassifies the existing registry using the robotics-first policy. It does not add papers, download or delete PDFs, or change paper notes. The canonical change is in `work/scripts/build_reading_tiers.py`; `READING_PLAN.md`, `READING_TIERS.csv`, `READING_STATUS.csv`, and generated synthesis queues are derived artifacts.

- Planning and active perception: `pr-0767` Lookahead Exploration with Neural Radiance Representation for Continuous Vision-Language Navigation; `pr-0540` VLFM; `pr-0768` Volumetric Environment Representation for Vision-Language Navigation; `pr-0230` IGL-Nav; `pr-0786` Move to Understand a 3D Scene.

- World models, safety, and recovery: `pr-0279` WMNav; `pr-0699` RoboDreamer; `pr-0700` Learning Interactive Real-World Simulators; `pr-0672` SafeMimic; `pr-0687` Ctrl-World.
- Whole-body, locomotion, and mobile/aerial manipulation: `pr-0604` AMO; `pr-0673` MOSART; `pr-0609` HOMIE; `pr-0743` Flying Hand; `pr-0578` SPIN; `pr-0603` WoCoCo.

### `ARCHIVE → NEXT` (2)

- `pr-0288` FOCI: trajectory optimization is performed directly on Gaussian splats and evaluated for robot motion planning.
- `pr-0810` SIMPACT: simulation-enabled VLM action planning is evaluated on real rigid and deformable manipulation tasks.

### `NEXT → REFERENCE` (3)

- `pr-0056` VGGT, `pr-0757` Open3DSG, and `pr-0794` EmbodiedSplat remain valuable 3D foundations, but their reported evaluations do not establish a robot behavior/control contribution sufficient for the current `NEXT` queue.

### `ARCHIVE → REFERENCE` (4)

- `pr-0402` CG-SLAM: dense RGB-D SLAM/state estimation foundation.
- `pr-0369` VarSplat: uncertainty-aware Gaussian-splat SLAM/state estimation.
- `pr-0233` PhysSplat: physics simulation over Gaussian-splat scenes; relevant enabling work, but no direct robot behavior evaluation.
- `pr-0463` Flow Equivariant World Models: action-conditioned world modeling and planning foundation, with no real-robot deployment in the verified evidence.

The pure reconstruction/rendering archive remains separated from robotics-first reading: `pr-0312`, `pr-0386`, `pr-0337`, `pr-0423`, `pr-0361`, `pr-0431`, `pr-0310`, `pr-0382`, `pr-0410`, `pr-0755`, `pr-0225`, `pr-0776`, `pr-0790`, and `pr-0807`. `WorldSplat` remains in `ARCHIVE` pending evidence of a closed-loop robot or driving behavior contribution.
