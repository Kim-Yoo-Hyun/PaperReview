## Main Claims
- A comprehensive user study indicates significant improvement in teleoperation capability over the AnyTeleop framework.
- We demonstrate the versatility of O PEN T EACH across 38 tasks on different robots.
- Further experiments exhibit that the collected data is compatible with policy learning on 10 dexterous and contactrich manipulation tasks.
